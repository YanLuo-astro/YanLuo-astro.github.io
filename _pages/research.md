## Starspots and lithium dispersion

I study how starspots contribute to the observed dispersion in lithium line strengths among young stars. Using spectroscopy and two-temperature photospheric models, I investigate how cool spots affect the Li I 6708 Å line and its interpretation as an indicator of lithium abundance and stellar age.

## Pre-main-sequence evolution with Gaia

I use Gaia data to test pre-main-sequence stellar evolutionary models, focusing on stellar ages and masses. My current work explores young stellar populations and wide binaries as benchmarks for assessing the consistency of model-inferred ages.
